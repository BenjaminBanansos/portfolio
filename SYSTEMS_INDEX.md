## 🎨 Interactive Features in the Portfolio Prototype

1. **Instant Category Filtering:** Visitors and hiring managers can filter between *All Systems*, *eCommerce & Custom Engines*, *Hospitality & Restaurants*, and *CleanTech, Health & Non-Profit*.
2. **Interactive Deep-Dive Modals:** Clicking any card opens a modal detailing:
   * The Client Profile and Benjamin's specific role
   * The Key Impact / Metric
   * The Technical or Commercial Challenge
   * The Architecture and Solution Delivered
   * Full Tag Cloud of APIs and Tools used
3. **Career Trajectory & Track Record:** Chronological timeline linking retail cash auditing (FreshCo), customer escalations (Millennium1), and full-scale digital consulting (Go Digitally).
4. **Three-Pillar Technical Matrix:** Clear breakdown of *eCommerce & Platforms*, *APIs & Cloud Plumbing*, and *Delivery & Product Governance*.
5. **Direct Hiring Action Banner:** High-converting contact callout with direct email, Canadian location, and remote availability.
